System sans, quiet hierarchy

Use the installed system font. Light display numerals; regular controls; tabular digits for timers and calls. No web-font download. Status words are omitted when the icon and value suffice.

03 / Touch & motion

Room to move.

Small visible icons inside generous touch targets. Short transitions for direct input. The actual LiveKit Aura supplies organic motion at the center, with a static resting state.

04 / Component family

One purpose at a time.

Presence & clock

A large circular wake target, nonselectable clock and sharper LiveKit aura. Nine simulated states remain in the lower-left simulator. Listening adds a faint white edge; mute uses a red edge and explicit icon.

Ephemeral device toast

Results toast at the lower left and fade after 4.2 seconds. The lower-right intent receipt opens the last 30 requests, named parameters and simulated Home Assistant or Spotify payloads.

Direct thermostat

The center and arc represent room temperature. The pointer represents the target. Rotate the pointer or tap either side for ±1°C. Red means above target; blue means below. Five idle seconds, then five seconds of an edge stroke before closing.

Reminder count

3

The top-right count opens and closes home editing. After 30 seconds, only pinned entries remain: right-aligned text with a number on the right. No heading, pin or drag handle remains in the quiet view. The accordion belongs only to control center.

Call presence

Avatar, duration and gentle activity bars replace “Return to call.” Tap to restore the full call view. The camera preview is a clearly marked local simulation.

Time & attention

Timers and alarms remain as small values below the aura. Timed alerts temporarily take the screen and pulse a strong white edge: long flash, short pause, two short flashes, long pause. Reduced motion uses a steady edge; every alert can be dismissed or snoozed.

06 / Implementation direction

Small enough to stay immediate.

Standalone HTML for review; a static Vite export for the Pi. A reusable Svelte 5 attachment owns the WebGL canvas and cleanup. The control center uses separate translucent glass cards over one black-tinted blur, with no enclosing panel or title. Microphone and sound mute are independent. The microphone control is visible only on home. The music summary uses five faint, thin activity bars and a brighter traveling bottom-edge pulse; both pause with playback, with a static reduced-motion alternative. Keep the finished app split into Aura, Clock, DeviceToast, LightTray, ThermostatDial, TimeRail, ReminderForm, AttentionSurface and CallPresence components.

Compact square controls belong to control center; lights currently on have reactive glow/fill tiles with no room or percentage. Expanded lights retain horizontal rows with room and brightness. Icons toggle directly, text supports double tap, and horizontal swipes adjust brightness. The reminder accordion always occupies the second control column from the right, with two icon squares for the list and a new reminder. Each entry owns its pin and reorder handle. Home reminders are background-free and right aligned. Home editing closes after 30 seconds, leaving only pinned text and number labels. One right swipe reveals Mark as done from the original title position; a short deliberate flick or 80% travel completes only that reminder. Slow partial and cancelled gestures make no change. Reorder gestures never open the control center. Supported color modes determine the available RGB and white presets. Use a gateway adapter for validated intent slots and durable deadlines. A real camera path would relay the server’s RTSP input to a browser-supported format such as WebRTC. On-screen typing should invoke the kiosk’s virtual keyboard.

The control center closes after one minute, with the edge stroke during its final five seconds. Other transient controls retain the five-plus-five-second close. Alarm wheels support held touch and mouse drags, scroll wheels, taps and keyboard input. The home temperature and weather sit below the date; a sensor picker inside the thermostat selects a simulated Home Assistant temperature entity without changing the target. Unavailable readings show a dash. Listening has a brighter gentle white flash; idle breath cycles in 5.5 seconds.

The shader uses at most 320 × 320 pixels and is capped at 24 fps while speaking, 20 fps otherwise. Idle shader rendering pauses while a subtle compositor-only breath remains. Hidden rendering and reduced-motion animation pause. These are rendering limits, not a Raspberry Pi benchmark. See the README and browser-check report for validation details.
