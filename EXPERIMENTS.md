## Governing rule and how to interpret the results

**Strictly no ASR/STT pipeline.** Local, small, finite-word, phonetic, optional and downstream transcription readers are not exceptions. A transcript-then-parse path defeats the direct VCM objective. Historical transcription studies below are retained for evidence and lessons only; their older recommendations do not override the current requirement. The historical STT brightness path was a compliance gap; E12 removes it from current inference and packaging while retaining its research evidence.

Finite command classification, acoustic parameter heads and direct audio/entity embeddings are distinct from transcription. Existing ordered-cue CTC experiments are described by their actual architecture and remain subject to the strict requirement: a fixed vocabulary or the word "keyword" alone does not certify that a reader avoids transcription. Do not approve a separate token-to-text parser as pure VCM. This index does not authorize a fallback or new transcription experiment.

Most command experiments use nine families: `POWER_ON`, `POWER_OFF`, `BRIGHTNESS`, `COLOR_SET`, `MEDIA_PLAY`, `MEDIA_RESUME`, `TIME_QUERY`, `DATE_QUERY`, `VOLUME`, plus `UNKNOWN`. That is **not** coverage of the ten assignment use cases. Timers, alarms, thermostat, weather/general queries, reminders/lists and calls/messages remain gaps in the inspected installed vocabulary. The relevant use cases are UC-01/02/03/04/08; name studies are conditional UC-01 extensions. See the requirements gap map before adding claims.

Keep three result levels distinct: raw acoustic recognition; accepted complete-command precision/recall after gates; and endpointed replay. Conditional parameter retrieval receives the role from annotations and is not full VCM accuracy. All latency below is desktop CPU evidence unless explicitly stated otherwise. No study listed here establishes a physical Pi or live-microphone acceptance result.

### Training, acceptance and demo boundary

**Hypothesis:** with balanced, naturally connected numeric/playlist supervision, a single small encoder can improve exact accepted-command recall at fixed precision/rejection requirements without a transcription stage. Train from random weights under one frozen recipe, initially about 30 epochs with early stopping and three declared seeds. Use balanced intent/parameter sampling so isolated words, background speech and volume examples do not dominate. Start with this one architecture; retain E02's frozen attention/shared controls and compare them only on the new benchmark's compatible command subset. Differences against the old headline scores alone cannot establish improvement.

Development selects checkpoint, thresholds, catalog policy and precision format before a locked test. Report each required field and full command with denominators, known-command rejects, unknown-name false accepts, invalid-command false accepts and endpoint losses. Always score the whole utterance; annotation-supplied family, entity role or a gold audio crop is diagnostic only. Separate ordinary endpoint replay from continuous listening, wake detection and real microphone evidence.

**Proposed first release targets, to freeze before fitting:** at least 95% complete-command precision and 90% complete-command recall for every supported intent; at most 1% unknown/invalid/unknown-name false accepts with confidence intervals and sufficient test support. On the actual Pi4/5, aim for at most 200 ms p95 processing and 700 ms p95 speech-end-to-result, including the current 400 ms endpoint wait, with recognition-process peak RSS at most 200 MB and real-time factor below 1. Include the worst accepted-name/catalog workload, cold load and all runtime dependencies. These are engineering proposals, not measured results or numeric requirements from the assignment. A pooled pass cannot hide a disabled family or a poorly supported percentage.

Begin hardware validation with explicit activation/push-to-talk; always-listening use requires a separately evaluated local wake detector and playback/echo robustness, counted in total cost. Keep voice recognition offline. A future Home Assistant adapter maps validated fields to known entity capabilities and playlist IDs using [service calls](https://developers.home-assistant.io/docs/api/rest/); the selected media integration determines playback support. **Unsupported at proposal time:** trained playlist enrollment, reliable natural 0â€“100 commands, full media operations, the missing timer/alarm/thermostat/query/reminder/contact categories, arbitrary reminder/message text, wake robustness and physical Pi acceptance. ## Cross-experiment conclusions and next work

1. **Keep the direct VCM objective.** Historical speech-recognition readers are excluded even when small or local. Fixed acoustic heads and non-transcribing embeddings still need complete-command and rejection evidence; fixed cue experiments need a functional compliance audit before reuse.
2. **Connected numbers remain a central gap.** More classes, isolated words, constructed percentages, FSC context and larger heads have not established natural 0â€“100 recognition. Preserve the current artifact until a separately authorized compliant replacement passes declared gates; do not promote from pooled metrics or software checks.
