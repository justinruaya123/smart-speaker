## Review focus

1. Changed model/catalog/policy or malformed CSV must not inherit previous acceptance authority.
2. Mid-utterance reload, dropped/out-of-order chunks, overflow and disconnect must never emit a stale accepted command.
3. Dynamic duplicate names/artists and unknown words must not silently resolve to an arbitrary ID.
4. Packaging must run without source checkout, corpus/cache, PyTorch or CUDA; check safe paths and complete wheel closure.
5. A successful software fixture or selective demo must not become a human/Pi/all-command qualification claim.
