Presenter notes · 0:00–1:00

The command model maps speech directly to an action and its values. The wake keyword is a separate stage. The causal encoder still waits for the full utterance before making its decision. We trained on human recordings and synthetic voices. The verified fit ran on a 3080, not an A100. We have no Raspberry Pi 4 measurements to report.
